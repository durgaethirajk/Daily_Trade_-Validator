Ratio guide

  • Gartley: AB/XA ≈ 0.618; BC/AB 0.382–0.886; AD/XA ≈ 0.786.
  • Bat: AB/XA 0.382–0.50; BC/AB 0.382–0.886; AD/XA ≈ 0.886.
  • Butterfly: AB/XA ≈ 0.786; BC/AB 0.382–0.886; AD/XA ≈ 1.27–1.618.
  • Crab: AB/XA 0.382–0.618; BC/AB 0.382–0.886; AD/XA ≈ 1.618.

A valid ratio match is only a candidate. Wait for price to reach the PRZ and confirm reversal structure before considering a trade.
